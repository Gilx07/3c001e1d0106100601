ClipFactory

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-09-04

1. Scope and current status

This policy describes how ClipFactory handles information in its user-authorized short-form video preparation and review workflow. The current local-safe TikTok publisher supports queueing and dry-run review; it does not currently perform TikTok OAuth, upload, or publication. Future OAuth-enabled behavior is described as future behavior and requires separate implementation and authorization.

2. Information used

  • Workspace and content data: source and derived video artifacts, transcripts, captions, titles, technical metadata, quality-control evidence, approval records, revision lineage, and publishing status when you ask ClipFactory to process or review them.
  • Connected-account data: in a future approved TikTok connection, the account identifier, username or profile information, granted scopes, creator options, and authorization status returned or required by the approved integration.
  • Operational records: bounded audit events, idempotency/state information, error classes, and timestamps needed for security, recovery, duplicate prevention, and support.
  • Credentials: OAuth client secrets, access tokens, refresh tokens, authorization codes, and temporary upload material are protected secrets. They are not intended for the browser, chat, Git, ordinary logs, or this policy.

3. Purposes

We use this information to provide requested transcription, clip preparation, quality-control review, authorization checks, publishing previews, user-approved platform operations when enabled, status tracking, security, troubleshooting, recovery, and compliance.

4. Authorization and user choices

A connected TikTok account would be authorized by its account holder through the platform’s supported OAuth flow. ClipFactory would request only approved scopes needed for the selected feature, show the target account and publishing options for review, and keep approval-required behavior as the default. You may decline authorization, disconnect the account, revoke platform access, or cancel eligible queued work.

5. TikTok and other third parties

If you authorize a future TikTok integration, TikTok will process information under its own terms and privacy practices. ClipFactory sends only the approved content and metadata needed for the requested operation. Platform responses, visibility restrictions, retention, and account controls remain subject to TikTok’s policies. No TikTok approval, public-posting access, or certification is claimed here.

6. Sharing and sale

ClipFactory is not designed to sell personal information. We do not describe a data-broker or advertising use in the current architecture. Information may be sent to a connected platform only when you request and authorize the applicable operation, or disclosed when required for security, law, or a legitimate service operation.

7. Retention and deletion

We retain content, approvals, status, and audit records only as needed for the requested workflow, recovery, security, legal obligations, or a documented retention policy. Deleting a workspace or revoking a connection should stop future use; it may not remove records required for integrity, dispute handling, or legal compliance. Temporary transfer material should expire and be cleaned up after reconciliation.

8. Security

ClipFactory uses server-side authorization boundaries, protected credential handling, least-privilege access, read-only projections for the Control Center, and redacted operational logging as applicable to the deployed feature. No security method eliminates all risk. Never send passwords, tokens, authorization codes, cookies, or private keys to ClipFactory chat or support.

9. Rights and contact

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing. Contact: support@dolenthis.my.id.

10. Changes

We may update this policy when the service, connected-platform requirements, or applicable law changes. The effective date above will identify the current version.
